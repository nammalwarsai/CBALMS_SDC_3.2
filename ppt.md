### **Slide 2: The Challenge (Problem Statement)**
**Visual:** Icons representing paperwork, confusion, and time-wasting.
**Text:**
- Manual tracking is error-prone and time-consuming.
- Lack of transparency in leave balances.
- Administrative bottleneck for HR and managers.
**Speaker Script:** 
"In many organizations, managing attendance and leaves manually or through outdated spreadsheets leads to a lot of friction. Employees struggle to know their leave balances, HR spends hours reconciling attendance data, and managers face bottlenecks when approving time off. We needed a centralized, automated solution."

---

### **Slide 3: The Solution (Overview)**
**Visual:** High-level diagram showing Employees and Admins connecting to a central cloud system.
**Text:**
- Centralized, secure, and automated platform.
- Role-based access (Admin & Employee).
- Real-time data synchronization.
**Speaker Script:** 
"Enter CBALMS. We have built a secure, centralized platform that automates these everyday HR tasks. By providing distinct, role-based portals for both Employees and Administrators, the system ensures that everyone has exactly the tools and data they need, right at their fingertips, in real-time."

### **Slide 4: Key Features - Employee Experience**
**Visual:** Screenshots of the Employee Dashboard, Check-in button, and Leave Form.
**Text:**
- **One-Click Attendance:** Simple Check-in and Check-out.
- **Leave Management:** Apply for leaves and view real-time balances.
- **Personal Dashboard:** Visual charts, attendance history, and stats.
- **Profile Management:** Keep personal details up to date.
**Speaker Script:** 
"For the employees, the focus is on self-service and ease of use. Upon logging in, they are greeted by a personalized dashboard. They can clock in and out with a single click, view their attendance history through interactive charts, and easily apply for leaves while instantly seeing their available leave balances."

### **Slide 5: Key Features - Administrator Control**
**Visual:** Screenshots of the Admin Dashboard, Employee Table, and Leave Approval screen.
**Text:**
- **Centralized Dashboard:** High-level metrics and statistics.
- **Employee Management:** Add, edit, and monitor staff.
- **Leave Workflow:** Approve or reject leave applications seamlessly.
- **Holiday Management:** Configure company-wide holiday calendars.
**Speaker Script:** 
"For administrators and HR, CBALMS provides complete control. The Admin Dashboard offers a bird's-eye view of today's attendance and pending requests. Admins can manage the employee directory, process leave applications with a click, and configure the company's holiday calendar, ensuring everyone is on the same page."

---

### **Slide 6: Advanced Capabilities & Automation**
**Visual:** Icons for Automation, Email, and Reports.
**Text:**
- **Auto-Checkout System:** Automatically clocks out employees who forget.
- **Smart Notifications:** Email alerts for logins, logouts, and leave status updates.
- **Reporting:** Exportable data and PDF reports.
- **Secure Authentication:** Robust login, signup, and password recovery.
**Speaker Script:** 
"Beyond the basics, we've integrated smart automation. If an employee forgets to clock out, our automated background script handles it for them. The system also features a robust notification engine, sending automated email confirmations for security and leave updates. Plus, admins can generate PDF reports for payroll and compliance."
